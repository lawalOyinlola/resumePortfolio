- The names `Lawal Oyinlola`, `Oyinlola Lawal`, `lawaloyinlola`, and `lawaloyinlola.com`

## You do not need permission to

- Use the code and the writing under the MIT license
- Say in plain text that your work is based on, forked from, or inspired by this repository
- Link to [resume.lawaloyinlola.com](https://resume.lawaloyinlola.com)
- Show screenshots of the site in articles, reviews, videos, and talks

## You do need permission to

- Use my name, mark, or wordmark as the identity of your own site, product, or organization
- Put any of them in a domain name, app name, social handle, or logo
- Show them in a way that suggests I built, endorse, or maintain your project

## If you fork this repository

Replace the branding and the personal data before you publish:

- `src/components/oyinn-mark.tsx` and `src/features/profile/components/oyinn-mark-isometric.tsx` (the "LO" mark)
- `src/components/site-footer-brand.tsx` (the "LAWAL" wordmark)
- `src/features/profile/data/` (my personal details)
- `src/config/site.ts` (site URL, résumé links, GitHub repository, UTM parameters)
- `src/app/manifest.ts` and `public/icons/` (app name and icons)
- `public/images/`, `public/resume/`, and `public/audio/lawal_oyinlola_pronounciation.mp3`
